HEY ALL HACKATHON/BUILDATHON JUDGES! This tool is a superb problem-solver, meant exclusively for you. It checks if the projects made have a significant human contribution involved in them or are they just a bundle of AI-generated code.

Paste a repository link, a representative code sample, and the commit count. CodePulse reads the sample for the fingerprints of real iteration — messy edges, uneven formatting, a believable commit history — and tells you whether there's a human pulse behind it, or a flatline.
